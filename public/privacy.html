While you design your invitation

Everything you type and every photo or song you add stays in your own web browser. It is not sent to our servers. Your browser keeps a draft so your work is not lost if you close the page, and you can clear it at any time with "Start a new invite" or by clearing your browser's site data.

When you pay

Payments are handled by our payment provider, [Stripe / Razorpay]. Card and bank details go directly to them and never reach us. To create the payment we send them:

  • the couple's names, shown on the payment page and your receipt, and
  • a one-way code made from the names and wedding date, used to unlock the right invitation.

The payment provider collects the details it needs to take the payment and send a receipt, such as your email address and billing country, under its own privacy policy. We can see those payment records in our account with them.

After you pay

Your browser stores a small unlock code so you can download your invitation again. Your finished invitation is a file that you keep and share yourself.

Your guests

Anyone who receives your invitation can see what you put in it. If you add a WhatsApp number for replies, anyone with the invitation can see that number. When a guest taps Maps, Directions, Add to calendar or a WhatsApp reply button, that service (Google or WhatsApp) opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Hosting and technical data

This site is hosted by Netlify, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and keep them secure. Our fonts are served from our own site, not from third parties. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use analytics.
