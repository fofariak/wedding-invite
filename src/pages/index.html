Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. You can design your whole invitation and see it on the phone preview for free, for as long as you like. The preview carries a light "PREVIEW" mark until you pay.

Do I need to create an account?

No. While you design, your draft is saved in your browser. After you pay, you get a private edit link that opens your invitation on any phone or computer. Keep it safe, like a password.

What do I get when I pay?

Your invitation is published at its own link, without the preview mark, ready to share on WhatsApp or anywhere else. You also get a private edit link for making changes later.

Can I make changes after paying?

Yes. Open your private edit link, change anything, including dates, functions, photos and the song, and press Save. Your guests see the update at the same link within a minute.

Who can see our invitation?

Anyone you send the link to, and anyone they forward it to. It is not password-protected, but it is not listed by search engines. You can unpublish or delete it at any time, and it goes offline automatically 12 months after the wedding date.

Do my guests need an app?

No. The invitation opens in the phone's web browser, on iPhone and Android, and on computers too.

How do guests reply?

Add a WhatsApp number in the editor and the invitation shows reply buttons. When a guest taps one, WhatsApp opens with a ready message to you. If you leave the number out, the reply section is hidden.

Can I add our own photos and music?

Yes. Add your own photos with captions, and a short song that plays as the book opens. Please use only photos and music you have the right to use.

Which traditions and languages are available?

Hindu, Nikah and Western designs today, each in three colour stories. The invitation's built-in words are in English, and everything you type, such as names, wording and functions, can be in any language. More designs, traditions and languages are coming.

Can I get a refund?

Because you can see the complete invitation before paying, payments are non-refundable. If you are charged twice or your invitation does not unlock, we will put it right. See the refund policy.
