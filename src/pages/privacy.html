While you design your invitation

Everything you type and every photo or song you add stays in your own web browser while you design. Nothing is sent to us until you publish. Your browser keeps a draft so your work is not lost if you close the page.

When you pay

Payments are handled by Razorpay. When you press Pay, Razorpay's payment window opens on our page and collects the details it needs, such as your phone number or email and your payment method, under its own privacy policy. Card, UPI and bank details go directly to Razorpay and never reach us. We receive the payment's status, amount and reference, which we keep with your invitation.

When you publish

To show your invitation to your guests, we store on our hosting provider's servers:

  • the details you entered (names, families, dates, functions, venue, wording, WhatsApp number if you added one),
  • the photos and song you added, and
  • the payment reference and when the invitation was created and last changed.

Your invitation gets a link with a random code. Anyone with that link can open it, so share it only with people you want to invite. Invitation pages ask search engines not to list them.

You also get a private edit link. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, so we cannot see or recover it. Your browser remembers it for you on that device.

Your guests

Guests can see everything in your invitation, including your WhatsApp number if you added one. Opening an invitation does not create an account or a guest list, and we do not record who opened it. When a guest taps Maps, Directions, Add to calendar or a WhatsApp reply button, that service (Google or WhatsApp) opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Hosting and technical data

This site and published invitations are hosted by Netlify, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep them secure. Our fonts are served from our own site. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use analytics.

Deleting your invitation

You can unpublish or delete your invitation at any time with your edit link. Deleting removes its details, photos and song from our storage straight away; copies held briefly by the delivery network expire within a day.
