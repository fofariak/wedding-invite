# Invite translations: phase 1 draft for review

Created using Anthropic Claude. This is a machine draft. Keep it internal until a native speaker has reviewed and corrected each language.

## For reviewers

These are the fixed words that appear on every animated wedding invitation sold on the site. Couples type their own names, venues and messages; everything below is the wording around them.

Please:
1. Correct anything that is wrong, unnatural, or too informal for a wedding invitation. Write your correction next to the draft.
2. Keep labels short where marked **(short)**. They sit on small buttons or under numbers on a phone screen.
3. Answer the questions in "Decisions" at the end.

`{name}`, `{date}`, `{time}`, `{rite}` and `{venue}` are filled in automatically. Leave them in place, but move them if your language needs a different word order.

Hindu invitations offer **Hindi, Gujarati and Marathi**. Muslim (Nikah) and Western invitations offer **Spanish, Portuguese, German and French**. English remains available for every tradition.

## Decisions for reviewers and the owner

1. **Formality.** The drafts use the polite form: Spanish *usted*, German *Sie*, French *vous*, Portuguese *você*. Modern Spanish and Portuguese invitations sometimes use the friendlier *tú*. Which suits your customers?
2. **Portuguese.** The drafts follow Brazilian spelling (*Cerimônia*). Portugal uses *Cerimónia* and slightly different wording. Which market comes first?
3. **Gendered words.** "Dear guest" (row 33) is masculine in Spanish, Portuguese and French. Options: keep it, use a neutral alternative, or show nothing when the guest leaves the name empty.
4. **Numbers.** The drafts keep 0–9 digits in Hindi, Gujarati and Marathi, as most modern Indian invites do. Native digits (०१२ / ૦૧૨) are possible.
5. **Rite word.** Gujarati uses *હસ્તમેળાપ* (Hastmelap) and Marathi *सप्तपदी* (Saptapadi) rather than "Pheras". Confirm these suit your customers.
6. **Couple joiner.** Hindi and Gujarati use *संग / સંગ*, and Marathi *आणि*. Alternatives include a heart symbol or keeping "Weds".
